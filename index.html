Yan Wu

I am a Ph.D. student at the Computer Vision and Learning Group, ETH Zürich, supervised by Prof. Siyu Tang. Before that, I completed my master’s degree at ETH Zürich and worked closely with Prof. Zhiwu Huang, Prof. Siyu Tang, and Dr. Suryansh Kumar. I received bachelor’s degrees through a double-degree program at Tongji University and the University of Bologna.

My research explores physics-based character animation and humanoid control, and vision-based policy learning.
